Terms of Service

These terms govern reservations made through this website.

1. Who we are

Asbury St Parking is a family-run operation offering parking on two private residential properties on Asbury Street, Saint Paul, Minnesota, during the twelve days of the Minnesota State Fair. In these terms, "we" and "us" mean the property owners; "you" means the person making the reservation.

2. What you are buying

You are buying a revocable licence to park one motor vehicle on one of our properties during a stated window, on a stated day. You are not renting a specific, identified parking space, and you are not entering into a lease or a bailment.

Except for a whole-fair reservation, we assign the space on arrival. This lets us place larger vehicles where they fit and keep the yards usable. A whole-fair reservation is for the named space shown at the time of booking.

4. Arrival

You must text us when you are about ten minutes away. We meet arriving vehicles on the street in front of the house; there is no booth, barrier or attendant on duty. If you arrive without notice we will do our best to accommodate you, but you may wait.

We hold your space for the window you booked. If you have not arrived and have not contacted us by the end of that window, the reservation is treated as a no-show under the refund policy below.

5. Vehicle size and condition

Spaces are residential driveway and yard spaces. We may refuse or relocate a vehicle that will not safely fit, including oversized pickups, vans, trailers, box trucks and recreational vehicles. This is why we ask for the make, model and colour at booking. If we refuse your vehicle on size grounds you receive a full refund, including the booking fee.

Vehicles must be roadworthy, currently licensed, and free of leaks. No overnight occupancy of vehicles. No trailers, generators, grills or open flames.

6. You park and keep your keys

You retain possession and control of your vehicle and its keys at all times. We do not take custody of your vehicle. No bailment is created. You park at your own risk.

7. Limitation of liability

To the fullest extent permitted by Minnesota law, we are not liable for theft of, damage to, or loss of any vehicle or its contents, however caused, including damage arising from weather, hail, falling branches, other vehicles, or the acts of third parties. You are responsible for locking your vehicle and removing valuables.

Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded, including liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence.

Where liability cannot be excluded, our total liability arising out of any reservation is limited to the amount you paid for that reservation.

8. Conduct on the property

These are private homes with families living in them. Please keep noise down, take your litter, do not block driveways or sidewalks, and follow directions from whoever is working the yard. We may cancel a reservation and ask a vehicle to leave, without refund, for abusive behaviour or for conduct that endangers people or property.

9. Price, tax and the booking fee

All prices shown include Minnesota sales tax. Parking is a taxable service in Minnesota and the tax is built into the advertised price rather than added at checkout.

A $2.00 online booking fee is added to each reservation. It is a charge for the reservation service — holding a space for you in advance, which a walk-up customer does not receive — and it applies to every online reservation regardless of card type. Sales tax is calculated on the total, including this fee.

10. If we cannot provide a space

If we cannot provide a space for a reservation we have accepted — for any reason, including flooding, a blocked yard, or our own error — you receive a full refund including the booking fee. That is the limit of our obligation; we are not liable for your onward costs, alternative parking, or admission tickets.

If the Minnesota State Fair itself does not open on your booked day, the same full refund applies.

11. Changes

We may change these terms for future reservations. The terms that apply to your reservation are those published at the time you booked. We keep dated copies.

12. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Minnesota. Disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts sitting in Ramsey County, Minnesota.

How to cancel. Text or email us using the details in the footer, with the name and day on the reservation. The time we receive your message is the time of cancellation.

How refunds are paid. Through Square, to the original card. We start the refund within two business days of agreeing it; your bank usually posts it within five to ten business days after that. We cannot refund to a different card or by cash.

Before disputing a charge, please contact us. Chargebacks take weeks and cost us a fee even when resolved in your favour; almost everything is faster to fix directly.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Your name — so we know who we are meeting.
  • Your mobile number — so we can reach you on the day, and so we recognise your arrival text.
  • Your estimated arrival time — so we hold the space at the right end of the day.
  • Your vehicle's make, model and colour — so we can assign a space it fits and identify it in the yard.
  • The reservation itself — day, option and amount paid.
We never see your card details. Payment is taken on Square's own checkout page. Your card number does not pass through this website and we do not store it. We receive only confirmation that a payment succeeded, and the last four digits on Square's receipt.

Why we collect it

Solely to fulfil your reservation and to contact you about it. We do not profile you, we do not build an advertising audience, and we do not sell or share your information with anyone for their own marketing.

Text messages

Giving us your number lets us text you about your reservation — for example, to confirm details or tell you about a problem on the day. We will not send you promotional messages unless you separately opt in, and if you do you can stop them at any time by replying STOP. Message and data rates from your carrier may apply.

How long we keep it

Reservation details, including your phone number and vehicle description, are deleted within 90 days of the end of the fair they relate to. We keep payment and sales-tax records for as long as Minnesota and federal law require, but those records do not include your vehicle description or phone number.

Your choices

Write to us and we will tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. If you ask us to delete a reservation that has not yet happened, we will treat that as a cancellation under the refund policy above, because we cannot hold a space for someone we can no longer identify or contact.

Children

This site is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Security

Reservations are held in a private database that is not publicly readable. The website is served over HTTPS. We are a small family operation, not a security company — we keep what we collect to the minimum and delete it promptly, which is the most effective protection we can offer.
